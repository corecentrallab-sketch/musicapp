## Short description (80 characters max)

## Full description
NoteSnap is the fastest way to go from hearing a song to playing it. Hold up your phone and NoteSnap identifies music playing around you, then helps you find the sheet music. It is Shazam built for musicians who want to play, not just listen.

RECOGNIZE ANY SONG
From Bach to Billie Eilish, NoteSnap recognizes classical public-domain works and modern songs. Public-domain pieces can include free sheet music. For copyrighted songs, NoteSnap links you directly to licensed retailers such as Musicnotes and Sheet Music Plus for a legal purchase.

SHEET MUSIC FOR PRACTICE
Read piano sheet music and guitar tablature in a clean, responsive reader. Use tap-to-turn pages, BPM-linked auto-scroll, and flexible two-page, half-page, and fit-width layouts.

PRACTICE TOOLS
Slow difficult passages with time-stretching without changing pitch. Loop sections, transpose keys, and correct automated transcription errors in the built-in notation editor.

EXPORT AND SYNC
Export MIDI, MusicXML, PDF, and GuitarPro. Import PDFs, scan physical scores, and sync your library across Dropbox, Google Drive, and other cloud services.

SMART, TRANSPARENT DISCOVERY
Personalized recommendations explain why each piece appeared. NoteSnap refreshes recommendations and surfaces niche works alongside familiar favorites.

BUILT FOR MUSICIANS
Daily streaks, weekly goals, achievement badges, and a daily challenge help turn listening into active practice. No pop-ups or interruptions while playing or viewing sheet music.

HONEST PRICING
The free tier includes five recognitions per month plus editor and practice tools. Pro adds unlimited recognitions, cloud sync, advanced recommendations, and custom skins, with a free trial. Cancel any time with one tap. See pricing and terms before subscribing.
