## Description
NoteSnap turns the moment you hear a song into a moment you can play. Identify music around you and find the sheet music you need — like Shazam, built for musicians.

RECOGNIZE CLASSICAL AND MODERN MUSIC
From Bach to Billie Eilish, NoteSnap recognizes classical public-domain works and modern copyrighted songs. For public-domain pieces, find free sheet music where available. For copyrighted songs, NoteSnap takes you to licensed retailers such as Musicnotes and Sheet Music Plus so you can buy official arrangements legally.

A READER MADE FOR PRACTICE
View piano sheet music and guitar tablature with tap-to-turn pages, BPM-linked auto-scroll, and two-page, half-page, or fit-width layouts.

TOOLS THAT HELP YOU IMPROVE
Slow tricky passages with pitch-preserving time-stretching, loop sections, transpose keys, and edit automated transcriptions in the built-in notation editor.

YOUR MUSIC, YOUR WAY
Export MIDI, MusicXML, PDF, and GuitarPro. Import PDFs, scan physical scores, and sync your library across Dropbox, Google Drive, and more.

DISCOVER WITH TRANSPARENCY
Recommendations learn from your playing habits, refresh regularly, and surface lesser-known works. Every recommendation includes a plain-language explanation.

STAY MOTIVATED
Daily streaks, weekly goals, achievement badges, and a daily challenge make practice a habit. No ads or interruptions while you play or have sheet music open.

START FREE
The free tier includes five recognitions per month, the notation editor, practice tools, exports, and offline access to saved music. Pro offers unlimited recognitions, full history, cloud sync, advanced recommendations, and custom skins, with a free trial. Pricing and renewal terms are always shown clearly; cancel with one tap.
